# Task Brief

## Objective

- What exactly must be achieved?

## Deliverable

- What concrete artifact or outcome must exist at the end?

## Current state

- What already exists?
- What is broken, missing, or uncertain?

## Constraints

- technical constraints
- time or cost constraints
- style or behavior constraints
- things that must not change

## Success criteria

- required outcome 1
- required outcome 2
- required outcome 3

## Assumptions allowed

- assumption 1
- assumption 2

## Assumptions forbidden

- do not invent missing facts
- do not silently choose risky defaults
- ask or flag when a hidden decision matters

## Required behavior from the model

- ask clarifying questions only if they materially change the outcome
- if uncertain, say what is unknown
- before claiming success, provide verification evidence

## Evidence expected at the end

- test result
- runtime check
- diff summary
- source or log proof

## Memory trigger

- If this task reveals a durable lesson, update memory before marking done.
